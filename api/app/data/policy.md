# CoverWise Insurance Brokers — Service Terms (demo KB)

CoverWise is an insurance broker. We compare and sell plans from many insurers (see MARKET CATALOG) and help our customers with renewals and claims. The insurer named on the policy is the one that pays claims.

## Who we help
- **Customers** who bought a policy through CoverWise: policy details, renewal, add-ons, NCB and claims. We share policy details only after we find the customer in our records by their full name as it appears on the policy.
- **Anyone shopping** for car, bike, health, term life or travel cover: we recommend and compare plans from the MARKET CATALOG. Catalog premiums are indicative "starting from" prices; the final premium depends on the insurer's underwriting (age, vehicle, city, health history).

## Motor cover basics (car and bike)
- **Third-party**: mandatory by law. Covers damage or injury you cause to other people and their property. Does NOT cover your own vehicle.
- **Comprehensive**: third-party plus damage to your own vehicle from accidents, fire, theft, floods and riots.
- **Common add-ons**: zero depreciation, engine protection, roadside assistance, return to invoice, consumables cover.
- **Riding gear and personal belongings** (helmets, jackets, phones) are NOT covered by a standard motor policy. Some insurers sell a personal-belongings add-on; check the policy.
- **Not covered**: driving without a valid licence or under the influence, wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, racing or commercial use without the right cover, claims reported more than 30 days after the incident.
- **Compulsory deductible** (set by IRDAI) per own-damage claim: ₹1,000 for cars up to 1500cc, ₹2,000 for cars above 1500cc, ₹100 for two-wheelers.
- **No Claim Bonus (NCB)**: a discount on the own-damage premium for claim-free years (20% → 25% → 35% → 45% → 50%). It lapses if the policy is not renewed within 90 days of expiry. It belongs to the owner and can be transferred to a new vehicle.

## Renewals
- We remind customers 30, 15 and 3 days before expiry. Renewing before the end date keeps continuous cover and the NCB.
- Health and term policies must be renewed on time to keep waiting-period credit.

## Filing a claim through CoverWise
1. We need: policy number (format INS-12345), incident date, and a short description of what happened. Verified customers can say "my car" and we use the policy on record.
2. A claim reference (format CLM-XXXXXX) is issued immediately and the insurer is notified.
3. A surveyor contacts you within 2 business days. Cashless repair is available at the insurer's network garages; cashless hospitalisation at network hospitals.
4. Typical settlement: 7–10 business days after the survey.
5. Statuses: received → surveyor_assigned → approved / rejected → settled.

## What we never do
- We never guarantee claim approval — the insurer decides based on the policy terms.
- We never ask for card numbers, Aadhaar, OTPs or passwords in chat.

- Grievances: we reply within 14 days; unresolved complaints can go to the Insurance Ombudsman.
